About StudentHub

StudentHub is a student-run web portal created to help students manage their college life in one place, from events to academic profiles.

Our Mission

To make campus activities easy to discover and easy to join, while keeping the interface simple and accessible for everyone.

This project is developed and maintained by a small team of student developers.
